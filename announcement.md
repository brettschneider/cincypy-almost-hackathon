# CincyPy "Almost Hackathon" — Announcement

> **Note:** Two flavor variants are sketched below — a longer Meetup.com post and a shorter LinkedIn post. Both intentionally avoid the actual challenge prompt. The problem is revealed at the start of the meeting.

### Why "Almost Hackathon"?

Because there isn't a competition to it. No prizes, no winners or losers. Just folks — solo or in small groups — working in the same room on the same project for an evening.

---

## Variant A — Meetup.com (longer)

**CincyPy "Almost Hackathon" — A Different Kind of Meetup**

*No prizes. No winners. No losers. Just folks — solo or in small groups — working in the same room on the same project.*

**Have you ever wanted to build an AI agent in Python?** This month's CincyPy is your chance.

Instead of our usual talk format, we're running a short, low-stakes, no-prizes hackathon-style session. Think of it as a creative show-and-tell with code.

**The project: an AI agent application written in Python.** We'll reveal the specifics at the meeting — what the agent does, what its inputs and outputs look like, what "done" means — but we want you to know the shape of it in advance so you can pick tools and arrive ready.

**When:** [DATE] — 6:30 PM to 8:30 PM (doors open at 6:00 PM)
**Where:** Pinnacle Solutions Group office, [ADDRESS] (our regular CincyPy venue, generously hosted by Pinnacle)
**Who:** Anyone who likes writing Python. No team required — come solo or pair up with someone you meet on the night.
**Cost:** Free. As always.
**Food:** [DETAIL — pizza / snacks / BYO — TBD with Steve / Pinnacle]

### What "Almost Hackathon" means

- **~2 hours total.** Roughly 90 minutes of build time, with the last 30 minutes reserved for anyone who'd like to show what they made.
- **No prizes, no judges, no contest.** This is about tinkering, learning, and seeing what folks come up with — not winning.
- **The problem gets revealed at the meeting.** You'll get it on the night, along with a handful of sample inputs to throw at your solution.
- **Python solutions.** Per tradition.
- **AI tools are fair game — and totally optional.** Use a frontier API key, run Ollama locally, or call Steve's no-auth Ollama instance on the office network — your call. You're not required to use any of them. And you're not required to skip them either. Pick whichever path (if any) you like.
- **AI-assisted dev tools are also optional.** Use Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor, Devin, or whatever you like — or don't. Your call.
- **Show-and-tell is optional.** If you'd rather just build and chat, that's totally fine too.

### Who this is for

Everyone. Experts, intermediates, and people still getting the hang of Python — all welcome. We're looking for creativity, not credentials. Coaches will be on hand during the build session to help anyone who wants a hand getting started or unstuck.

### What to bring

- A laptop with **Python 3.10+** installed.
- Your favorite editor.
- *(Optional)* An API key for a frontier model, OR a working local Ollama install, OR a willingness to use Steve's LAN endpoint. **All three are optional.**
- *(Optional)* An AI-assisted dev tool like Claude Code, Copilot, or Cursor — installed and authenticated. Also optional.
- We'll send out setup instructions before the meeting so you can get sorted ahead of time — that way we don't burn half the night on installs.
- A willingness to think a little creatively.

### Schedule (rough)

- **6:30 – 6:40 PM** Welcome, reveal the challenge, hand out sample inputs.
- **6:40 – 8:00 PM** Build time. Coaches (including Steve) will float around and help anyone who wants a second pair of eyes — or a first pair.
- **8:00 – 8:30 PM** Optional show-and-tell. Anyone who made something fun can demo it in a minute or two.
